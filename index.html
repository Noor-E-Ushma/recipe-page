Simple Omlette Recipe

An easy and quick dish perfect for any meal.this classic omlette combines beaten eggs cooked to perfection, optionally filled with your choice of cheese vegetables or meats.

preparation time
  • Total :approximately 10 minutes
  • preparation:5 minutes
  • cooking:5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2-3 large eggs
  • salt, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil
  • pepper to taste
  • optional fillings: cheese,diced vegetables cooked meats,herbs

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs: In the bowl beat the eggs until they are well mixed
  2. heat the pan: place a non stick pan over medium heat and add butter and oil
  3. cook the omlette: once the pan is heated pore in the eggs
  4. add fillingd(optional): when the eggs began to set at the edges but are still slightly running in the middle sprinkle your chosen filling
  5. Fold and serve: As the ommlette continues to cook, carefully lift one edge and fold it over the fillings
  6. Enjoy: serve hot with aditional salt and pepper if needed
